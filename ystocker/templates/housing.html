Asking vs Sold Price realtor.com Redfin

What sellers list at, against what buyers actually paid. Asking above sold means sellers have not yet met the market; the two converging is the market clearing.

Listing Supply realtor.com

Homes on the market against how many were newly listed each month. Active rising while new listings fall means what is listed is not selling.

Days on Market realtor.com

Median days a listing sits before going under contract. The 2021 trough and the climb since is the clearest single picture of the market cooling.

Demand Pressure realtor.com

Pending-to-active ratio and the share of listings that have taken a price cut. A falling ratio with a rising cut share is sellers losing pricing power.

Construction Pipeline FRED

Permits, starts and completions in thousands of units at an annual rate. They run in that order, so permits turning down is the earliest warning of a construction slowdown.

Months' Supply of New Homes FRED

How long the unsold new-home inventory would last at the current sales pace. Six months is the conventional balance point; above that, builders discount.

New Single-Family Home Sales FRED

Thousands of units at an annual rate, back to 1963. New homes are a small share of sales but the part that responds fastest to rates.

Inventory & New Listings Redfin

Homes for sale versus homes newly listed each month. Inventory rising while new listings flatten means buyers are absorbing less.

Who Has the Leverage Redfin

Share of listings cutting price against share selling above asking. When the lines cross, negotiating power changes hands.

Reading these two sources together

Zillow and Redfin do not measure the same market, so their price levels are not interchangeable. For the same month Redfin reported a median sale price of about $440k against Zillow's $370k — roughly a 19% gap — because Redfin's universe is MLS-centric and metro-weighted while Zillow's is broader. Their price-cut measures differ in definition too, not just in coverage. Each card above therefore names its publisher, and no chart mixes the two on one axis as though they agreed. Use Zillow for home-value and rent levels across metros, and Redfin for market mechanics: inventory, months of supply, days on market and the share selling above asking.

Both series are revised as late transactions arrive, and Zillow's home-value and sale-price indices are seasonally adjusted while several supply measures are not. Monthly data, updated once a month.
