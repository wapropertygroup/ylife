What it is它是什么

{{ product }} is a research desk made of language-model agents. You give it one ticker and one date. It seats a team of analysts to study that company as of that date, makes a bull and a bear argue over what they found, has a trader turn the verdict into a plan and a risk team attack the plan, and ends with a portfolio manager signing a rating.

The point is not to replace your judgment. It is to put a whole desk's worth of argument in front of you — every turn in the open, with the evidence each role worked from — so you can see where a conclusion came from, and where it is weakest.

{{ product }} 是一个由大语言模型智能体组成的投研台。你给它一只股票和一个日期，它会组建一支分析师团队，研究这家公司在那一天的状况；让看涨与看跌研究员就研究结果展开辩论；由交易员把结论变成交易计划，再由风险团队对计划发起质询；最后由投资组合经理签发评级。

它的目的不是替你做判断，而是把一整个投研团队的论证摆在你面前——每一轮发言都公开，并附上每个角色所依据的证据——让你看清结论从何而来，又在哪里最站不住脚。

What a run produces一次分析产出什么

  • A report written as a conversation — one turn per role, in the order the desk spoke, each with its own link.
  • A decision — exactly one of Buy, Overweight, Hold, Underweight or Sell, with the portfolio manager's reasoning and the price levels and sizing behind it.
  • A PDF of the whole report, charts included, and an email to you when the run finishes.
  • A link you can share with somebody who has no account, and take back later.
  • 一份以对话形式呈现的报告——每个角色一轮发言，按投研台的发言顺序排列，每一轮都有独立链接。
  • 一个决策——在买入、增持、持有、减持、卖出中五选一，附投资组合经理的理由，以及背后的价位与仓位建议。
  • 一份 PDF，包含完整报告与图表；分析完成后还会发邮件通知你。
  • 一个可分享的链接，对方无需账号即可打开，你也可以随时撤回。

Boundaries边界

{{ product }} is built for research. It places no orders, connects to no brokerage account and holds no money. A report is a model's argument, not investment advice: it can be wrong, and it can be confidently wrong. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything — the rating a run ends on is the desk's conclusion, and the decision is yours.

{{ product }} 为研究而生。它不下单、不连接任何券商账户，也不持有任何资金。报告是模型的论证，而不是投资建议：它可能出错，而且可能错得很自信。本站的任何内容都不构成买入或卖出任何资产的建议——一次分析最后给出的评级只是投研台的结论，决定权在你。

Core capabilities核心能力

  • {{ facts.n_base }} analysts on every run — market, sentiment, news, earnings, quality and valuation — and {{ facts.n_astock }} for China A-shares, which add policy, hot-money and lock-up specialists.
  • Structured debate — {{ facts.debate_rounds }} bull-versus-bear rounds and {{ facts.risk_rounds }} risk rounds by default, each argued from the analysts' reports.
  • Dated to the run — the data tools are asked for what was known on the run's date, so a report dated in the past is written from that day's information, as far as each source can supply it.
  • Streams as it works — each section appears on the page the moment its author finishes, so a long run can be read while it is still going.
  • Your choice of model and thinking depth, recorded on every report so a cheap-tier run never passes for the most capable one.
  • English or Chinese — the report, its PDF and the completion email are written in the language the run was started in.
  • 每次分析 {{ facts.n_base }} 位分析师——市场、情绪、新闻、盈利、质量与估值；A 股分析为 {{ facts.n_astock }} 位，额外加入政策、游资与解禁三位专项分析师。
  • 结构化辩论——默认进行 {{ facts.debate_rounds }} 轮多空辩论与 {{ facts.risk_rounds }} 轮风险辩论，每一轮都以分析师报告为依据。
  • 以分析日期为准——数据工具只被询问在分析日期当天已知的信息，因此一份日期在过去的报告，会在各数据源所能提供的范围内，基于当天的信息写成。
  • 边写边看——每个章节在作者写完的那一刻就出现在页面上，耗时较长的分析也可以边运行边阅读。
  • 可自选模型与思考深度，并记录在每份报告上，低价档位的分析不会被误认为最高档位。
  • 中文或英文——报告、PDF 和完成通知邮件都使用发起分析时选择的语言。
