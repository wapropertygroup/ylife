For the earnings, quality and valuation analysts, code makes the data calls and computes every figure; the model only interprets them, and says so when there is nothing to measure, such as a fund.
  2. Research debate. A bull and a bear researcher argue over those reports for {{ facts.debate_rounds }} rounds, one turn each per round, the bull first.
  3. Ruling. The research manager weighs the debate and hands the trader a plan: a recommendation on the same five-point scale as the final decision, the arguments that decided it, and concrete steps. It rereads the numeric reports itself, and is told that conflict alone is no reason to rate Hold.
  4. Proposal. The trader turns the plan into a trade: Buy, Hold or Sell, with — where it can state them — an entry, a stop and a target as prices in the quote currency, and a position size, anchored in the market analyst's price levels. On an A-share it is told to respect T+1 settlement, daily price limits and 100-share lots.
  5. Risk debate. Aggressive, conservative and neutral risk analysts attack the proposal in that order, one turn each per round for {{ facts.risk_rounds }} rounds.
  6. Decision. The portfolio manager reads the plan, the proposal and the whole risk debate, and signs the rating.

The two managers use the run's deep model and everyone else its quick model — see Models and thinking depth. The desk describes every seat.

  4. 交易方案。交易员把计划变成一笔交易：买入、持有或卖出；能够明确给出时，以报价币种的价格写出入场价、止损价和目标价，并给出仓位大小，价位以市场分析师报告中的价格结构为依据。对于 A 股，它被要求遵守 T+1 交收、涨跌停限制和 100 股一手的规则。
  5. 风险辩论。激进、保守、中立三位风险分析师按此顺序质询方案，每轮各发言一次，共 {{ facts.risk_rounds }} 轮。
  6. 决策。投资组合经理通读投资计划、交易方案和整场风险辩论，签发最终评级。

两位主管使用本次分析的深度模型，其余角色使用快速模型——详见模型与思考深度。每个席位的介绍见投研团队。

The decision最终决策

The portfolio manager signs exactly one of five ratings, defined for it as:

RatingMeaning
BuyStrong conviction to enter or add to a position
OverweightFavourable outlook; increase exposure gradually
HoldKeep the current position; no action needed
UnderweightReduce exposure; take partial profits
SellExit the position, or avoid entering

It comes with an executive summary and a thesis — what decided it, and what would change it — plus, where it can state them, a size, entry, stop, target and holding period. A final text with no readable rating shows as REVIEW, never as a silent Hold. The manager also sees how the desk's earlier calls on the same ticker turned out, once enough trading has passed to judge them.

投资组合经理在五档评级中签发唯一一个，各档含义如下：

评级含义
买入信心强，建仓或加仓
增持前景向好，逐步提高仓位
持有维持现有仓位，无需操作
减持降低仓位，部分获利了结
卖出清仓，或不建仓

评级附有执行摘要和投资逻辑——是什么决定了它，什么情况会改变它；能够明确给出时，还附上仓位、入场价、止损价、目标价和持有期限。最终文本里读不出评级时，会显示 REVIEW（待复核），绝不会悄悄当作“持有”。投资组合经理还能看到投研台过去对同一只股票的判断，在经过足够的交易日之后表现如何。

What else the desk is told数据之外的背景信息

Three notes go to the trader, the risk team and the portfolio manager — not to the analysts:

  • Market regime — a summary from this site's market dashboards: S&P 500 valuation against its history, market breadth, CTA positioning and the odds priced for Fed policy, labelled as describing the market rather than the company. Anything that could not be read is left out, not guessed.
  • Relative strength — when the ticker is in one of the site's peer groups, how its analyst-estimate revisions compare with a few peers', in Yahoo's own figures.
  • Your portfolio — holdings from the Assets page, read when the run starts and looked through funds to the companies inside, with any limits you set there. With a limit set, an arithmetic check between the trader and the risk debate keeps the proposed size, cuts it to the largest your limits and investable cash allow, or blocks the trade — when no size fits, or when the proposal's numbers are missing or inconsistent. A final size beyond that ruling is flagged on the report. A run that used your holdings never appears among the public sample reports. With no holdings, nothing is passed: the desk does not assume you start from zero.

有三类信息只提供给交易员、风险团队和投资组合经理，分析师看不到：

  • 市场环境——来自本站市场看板的摘要：标普 500 估值在历史中的位置、市场宽度、CTA 仓位，以及市场对美联储政策的定价概率，并注明描述的是大盘而非这家公司。读不到的数据直接略去，不做猜测。
  • 相对强弱——当该股票属于本站某个同业组时，给出它的分析师预期修正与少数同业的对比，数字直接取自 Yahoo。
  • 你的持仓——来自资产页面，在分析启动时读取，并穿透基金看到其中的公司，同时带上你在那里设定的上限。设定了上限时，交易员与风险辩论之间有一道纯算术的检查：维持原定仓位；削减到上限和可投现金所允许的最大仓位；或者否决这笔交易——当任何仓位都无法满足上限，或者方案数字缺失、自相矛盾时。最终仓位若超出这一裁定，报告会明确标注。使用过你持仓的分析，永远不会出现在公开的报告样例中。没有持仓时，系统不传入任何组合信息——投研台不会因此假定你从零仓位开始。

Dated to the run以分析日期为准

Every dated data tool takes its date from the run, so a model cannot ask a source for anything later — the first guard against look-ahead bias — and a date after today is refused at the door and costs nothing. Not every source can look back, though:

EvidenceOn a run dated in the past
Prices and indicatorsCut off at the analysis date.
News, StockTwits, RedditTrimmed to a window ending on the date. These feeds serve only recent items, so a window they never saw is reported as unavailable, not as “no news”.
FRED macro seriesAs they stood on the date, before later revisions.
Prediction-market oddsWithheld: Polymarket serves only live odds.
Earnings estimatesFrom snapshots the desk recorded on or before the date; with none, no numbers.
Quality and valuation figuresNot dated. Yahoo Finance's company snapshot has no history, so these are today's ratios and multiples.
A-share consensus, northbound flow, industry rankingsToday's snapshot, flagged as not being that day's.
Market-regime and peer notesThe market as the run starts, with their own dates.
Lessons from earlier callsOnly outcomes known by the date.

So the further back the date, the more a report rests on prices and macro data — and the more of its other sources say they could not see that day.

每个带日期的数据工具都从分析任务本身读取日期，模型无法向数据源索取更晚的数据——这是防范前视偏差的第一道关口；晚于今天的日期会在排队之前就被拒绝，不消耗次数。不过，并非每个数据源都能回溯：

证据分析日期在过去时
价格与技术指标截止到分析日期。
新闻、StockTwits、Reddit裁剪到截至该日期的时间窗口。这些数据源只提供近期内容，从未覆盖到的窗口会标为“不可用”，而不是“没有新闻”。
FRED 宏观数据采用当天的数据版本，不含之后的修订。
预测市场赔率不提供：Polymarket 只有实时赔率。
盈利预期读取投研台在该日期当天或之前记录的快照；没有快照就不给数字。
质量与估值数据不按日期回溯。Yahoo Finance 的公司快照没有历史版本，看到的是今天的比率与估值倍数。
A 股一致预期、北向资金、行业排名当前快照，并注明并非当天的版本。
市场环境与同业对比分析启动时的市场状况，标注各自数据的日期。
过往判断的经验只包含该日期之前已知结果的判断。

因此，日期越早，报告就越依赖价格与宏观数据，也会有越多的数据源说明自己看不到那一天。

Queueing, streaming and time limits排队、实时输出与时限

  • Queue. If the server is busy, your run shows as queued until a slot frees up; one that waits too long is cancelled and handed back.
  • Streaming. Each agent's turn is published as soon as it exists, so a long run can be read while it is still going.
  • Time limit. A run that passes the limit shown under the run form is stopped and marked as an error.
  • Interruptions. If a run is cut off after the agents have written — a server restart, say — the report is rebuilt from what had streamed and marked recovered from stream, since its final synthesis may be missing.
  • Email. A finished report is mailed to the address you signed in with, in its own language. A run that ends in an error sends nothing.
  • 排队。服务器繁忙时，你的任务会显示为排队，直到空出位置；等待过久的任务会被取消，并退还次数。
  • 实时输出。每位智能体的发言一生成就会发布，耗时较长的分析可以边运行边阅读。
  • 时限。超过表单下方所示时限的分析会被终止，并标记为出错。
  • 中断。如果分析在智能体已经写出内容之后被打断——比如服务器重启——系统会用已输出的内容重建报告，并标注由实时流恢复，因为最后的综合结论可能缺失。
  • 邮件。分析完成后，报告会以其撰写语言发送到你登录所用的邮箱。以出错告终的分析不发邮件。
