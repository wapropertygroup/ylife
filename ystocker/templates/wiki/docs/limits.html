Why there are limits为什么设有限额

Anyone with a Google account can sign in and start a run, and every run is paid for in model calls — a deep run on the default model makes dozens of calls to the most capable model. Without ceilings, a public sign-in button would be an open-ended bill.

A limit per person is not enough on its own, because there is no limit to how many Google accounts can sign in. So there is also a ceiling for the whole site, and it counts purchased runs too: it measures what the service can deliver in a day, not what has been paid for.

任何拥有 Google 账号的人都可以登录并发起分析，而每一次分析都要为模型调用付费——使用默认模型的一次深度分析，要调用最强的模型几十次。如果不设上限，一个公开的登录按钮就等于一张没有上限的账单。

仅按人设限还不够，因为能登录的 Google 账号数量没有上限。所以全站还有一个总上限，购买的次数同样计入其中：它衡量的是服务一天能交付多少，而不是有多少人付了费。

When counters reset计数何时重置

Every daily counter — your free runs, questions and shares, and the site-wide total — resets at {{ reset_en }}. A run is counted when you submit it, not when it finishes, so starting several at once does not get around the limit. A run that is given back returns to the day it was charged to, even if it failed after midnight. Purchased runs never reset: they carry over until you use them.

所有每日计数——你的免费分析、追问和分享次数，以及全站总数——都在{{ reset_zh }}重置。分析在提交时即计数，而不是在完成时，所以同时发起多个分析也绕不过上限。被退还的次数会回到当初扣除的那一天，即使分析是在午夜之后才失败的。购买的次数不会重置，会一直保留到你用完为止。

When a run is given back何时退还次数

  • Refused when you submit it — a ticker the form cannot accept, a malformed or future date, or a model that is not available: stopped before it is queued, and given back at once.
  • Failed before any model was called — it never got a slot, or the analysis could not start: given back.
  • Stopped because the model provider's daily capacity ran out, with no report: given back, even though some calls were made.
  • Failed after the models started work: not given back, because by then an unknown number of model calls have been paid for.
  • Broke near the end: the report is rebuilt from the sections already streamed, marked recovered from stream, and counts as a finished run.

A free run goes back to that day's allowance, and a purchased run goes back to your balance.

  • 提交时被拒绝——股票代码无法识别、日期格式有误或日期在未来，或所选模型不可用：在排队之前就被拒绝，并立即退还。
  • 在调用任何模型之前失败——一直没排到运行位置，或分析未能启动：退还。
  • 因模型服务商当日额度用尽而中止，且没有产出报告：退还，即使已经调用过一些模型。
  • 模型开始工作之后失败：不退还，因为此时已经为数量不明的模型调用付了费。
  • 在接近尾声时出错：报告会用已经实时输出的章节重建，标注为“由实时流恢复”，按已完成的分析计算。

免费次数退回当天的免费额度，购买的次数退回你的余额。

When you reach a limit达到上限时

  • Out of free runs — the page offers run packs, when they are on sale. See Pricing and credits.
  • Site-wide ceiling reached — the page says so and offers no pack, because buying a run cannot add capacity that is not there. Try again after the reset.
  • Out of questions or shares — they come back at the reset.
  • 免费次数用完——页面会提供次数包（在售时）。详见价格与次数。
  • 达到全站上限——页面会明确告知，而且不会推荐购买次数包，因为花钱也买不来并不存在的容量。请在重置后再试。
  • 追问或分享次数用完——重置后即可恢复。
