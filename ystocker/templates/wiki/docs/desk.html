Quality and valuation: two questions, two seats质量与估值：两个问题，两个席位

Until 30 August 2026 a single fundamentals analyst covered the company's books. It was split in two because “is this a good business?” and “is this a good price?” are different questions — the distinction Buffett and Munger draw — and one generic report kept blurring them: a fine business can still be an expensive stock.

  • Quality works from returns, margins, leverage, liquidity and cash generation, and how steady margins have been over several years.
  • Valuation works from trailing and forward P/E, PEG, price-to-book and dividend yield.

Both are built like the earnings analyst, which joined the desk on 19 September 2026: code fetches the evidence, computes every figure and assigns the tier, and the model may only add judgement around it — a moat assessment and red flags; what the valuation tier means and what could re-rate the multiple — without restating, rounding or correcting a single number. Reports written before the split still show their Fundamentals Analyst turn.

2026 年 8 月 30 日之前，公司的财务状况由一位基本面分析师统一负责。之后它被拆成两个席位，因为“这是不是一门好生意？”和“这是不是一个好价格？”是两个不同的问题——这正是巴菲特和芒格所强调的区分——而一份笼统的报告总是把两者混在一起：好公司的股票，照样可能很贵。

  • 质量依据回报率、利润率、杠杆、流动性与现金创造能力，以及多年来利润率是否稳定。
  • 估值依据滚动市盈率与预期市盈率、PEG、市净率和股息率。

两者的构造与 2026 年 9 月 19 日加入团队的盈利分析师相同：证据由代码获取，每一个数字由代码计算，评级档位也由代码给出；模型只能在此基础上补充判断——护城河评估与风险警示，或者估值档位意味着什么、哪些因素可能让估值倍数重估——不得复述、四舍五入或修改任何一个数字。拆分之前的旧报告里，仍然保留着基本面分析师的发言。

SeatReadsWrites

Bull and bear researchersEvery analyst report, and the debate so farAlternating arguments
Research managerThe debate, plus the earnings, quality, valuation and A-share reports directlyA rating and an investment plan
TraderThe plan, the market report, the earnings, quality and valuation reports, and the context notesA direction, price levels and a size
Risk teamThe proposal, every analyst report, the context notes and any portfolio-limit rulingA three-way debate
