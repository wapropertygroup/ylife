September 20262026 年 9 月

2026-09-26

  • Docs and Research Lab launched, with a new landing page for visitors who are not signed in.
  • A run dated in the future is refused before it is queued, so it costs nothing.
  • With the analysis engine on TradingAgents 0.5.1, a report dated in the past is no longer told today's date, or any date after the run.
  • 上线产品文档与研究实验室，并为未登录的访客推出全新首页。
  • 分析日期在未来的请求，会在排队之前就被拒绝，不消耗任何次数。
  • 分析引擎升级至 TradingAgents 0.5.1：日期在过去的报告，不会再被告知今天的日期或分析日之后的任何日期。

2026-09-19

  • Every run now includes an Earnings Analyst, who reads consensus estimates, how they have been revised and the company's record of surprises.
  • The analysis engine caught up with TradingAgents 0.5.0. For a report dated in the past, every data tool now takes the run's date, macro data is read as it was published at the time, and social posts are trimmed to the analysis window.
  • A news or social feed with nothing for the analysis window now says the data is unavailable, instead of passing for a quiet week.
  • A decision whose rating cannot be read now shows as REVIEW, rather than being recorded as Hold.
  • In each debate, the side that speaks first opens with its own case instead of rebutting an argument nobody had made yet.
  • 每次分析新增盈利预期修正分析师，解读一致预期、预期的修正轨迹，以及公司过往的业绩超预期记录。
  • 分析引擎同步至 TradingAgents 0.5.0。对于日期在过去的报告，所有数据工具都以分析日期为准，宏观数据按当时发布的版本读取，社交媒体帖子也只截取分析窗口内的内容。
  • 新闻或社交数据源在分析窗口内没有数据时，会明确注明数据不可用，不再被误读为“风平浪静”。
  • 无法读出评级的决策现在显示为 REVIEW，不再被记作“持有”。
  • 每轮辩论中先发言的一方，会先陈述自己的观点，不再去反驳一个对方还没有提出的论点。

  • If you keep holdings in My Assets, the decision agents now see them, and a proposed position size is checked against any concentration limits you have set there.
  • Share by text message: the dialog opens your phone's Messages app with the link ready.
