{% if sub.trial_days %}
How does the free trial work?免费试用如何运作？

Pick a plan and add a card. Nothing is charged for {{ sub.trial_days }} days; when they are up, the plan you picked starts and the card is charged, unless you cancelled in billing before then. Each account can have one free trial.选择方案并绑定银行卡。{{ sub.trial_days }} 天内不收任何费用；试用期满后，你选择的方案开始生效并扣费，除非你在此之前已在账单中取消。每个账号限试用一次。

{% endif %}
Can I cancel any time?可以随时取消吗？

Yes. Manage billing opens Stripe's billing page, where you can cancel, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card and download invoices. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.可以。“管理账单”会打开 Stripe 的账单页面，你可以在那里取消订阅、在月付与年付之间切换、更新银行卡并下载发票。取消后，方案会一直有效到已付费周期结束。

Are there discounts?有折扣吗？

The yearly plan costs {{ sub.saving_pct }}% less than twelve months of the monthly one. We also hand out promotion codes from time to time; enter one on Stripe's checkout page.年付方案比按月付十二个月便宜 {{ sub.saving_pct }}%。我们也会不定期发放优惠码，可在 Stripe 结账页面输入。

What happens to run packs I bought?已购买的次数包怎么办？

They stay on your account and never expire. With Pro, a purchased run is used only once the day's {{ sub.runs_per_day }} are gone.它们会保留在你的账号中，永不过期。开通 Pro 后，只有当天 {{ sub.runs_per_day }} 次用完，才会使用购买的次数。

Is there any other limit?还有其他限制吗？

The whole site runs at most {{ global_runs }} analyses a day across every account, a limit on what the server and its model quota can deliver. Subscribers count toward it like everyone else; when it is reached, runs start again after midnight Pacific.全站所有账号加起来每天最多运行 {{ global_runs }} 次分析，这是服务器和模型配额的容量上限。订阅用户同样计入；达到上限后，太平洋时间零点过后即可继续运行。
